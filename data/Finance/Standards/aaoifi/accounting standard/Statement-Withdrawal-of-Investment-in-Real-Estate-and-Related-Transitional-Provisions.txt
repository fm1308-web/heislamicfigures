AAOIFI Accounting Board (AAB) Statement 1/2025
Withdrawal of FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate” and Related
Transitional Provisions


Objective of the statement

The objective of this statement is to:
a.           withdraw AAOIFI Financial Accounting Standard (FAS) 26 “Investment in Real Estate”; and b.           provide necessary guidance (including transitional provisions) to the Islamic financial
institutions or other institutions applying AAOIFI FASs (the institutions) in respect of accounting and financial reporting for investment property, as applicable in addition to the generally accepted accounting principles, once FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate” stands withdrawn.

Statement of withdrawal

AAOIFI Accounting Board (AAB) has decided to withdraw FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate” (see paragraph 13), considering that:

a.           after revision of “AAOIFI Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting” and FAS 1 “General Presentation and Disclosures in the Financial Statements” there is a need for aligning the accounting and financial reporting requirements applicable in respect of investment property; and

b.           certain specific requirements related to the Shari’ah principles and rules and the business model of Islamic financial institutions contained in FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate” are already provided in other relevant AAOIFI FASs1.

Accounting and financial reporting guidance in respect of investment property Following the withdrawal of FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate”, an institution shall account for investment property and present the same in its financial statements in line with the requirements
of generally accepted accounting principles2, insofar as the same do not contradict with the
requirements of any other AAOIFI FAS(s), “AAOIFI Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting”
and Shari’ah principles and rules3.

Change in accounting policy
An institution shall assess at the time of withdrawal of FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate”, as to whether such withdrawal would result in a change in accounting policy for the institution, particularly considering the suitable business model applicable to the institution and / or applicable
in respect of its contractual arrangements with participatory stakeholders.4

A change in accounting policy shall be accounted for in line with the requirements of respective AAOIFI FAS5, subject to the specific requirements and transitional provisions prescribed in this statement. (See paragraphs 7-11)

Attribution of gains, losses and reserves
In case investment property (or an item thereof) is financed solely or jointly by participatory stakeholders other than common shareholders of an institution, the respective provisions of relevant AAOIFI FASs6 shall continue to apply, notwithstanding the withdrawal of FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate”. Such provisions require an institution to ensure that the relevant income, profits, gains, losses and reserves (including accumulated losses / negative reserves) are attributed between the relevant stakeholders in line with the requirements of Shari’ah principles and rules and contractual arrangements.

Transitional provisions
There is a rebuttable presumption that in most cases a constructive liquidation(s) has already taken place in respect of attributions to different participatory stakeholders other than common shareholders, hence, a full restatement application of the new accounting policy may not be
appropriate in all cases.

Irrespective of whether the new accounting policy is applied using a full restatement approach or a modified restatement approach, the balances attributable to the respective participatory stakeholders at the end of the last financial reporting period of application of FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate” shall remain unchanged (including, unrealised gains, losses and reserves (or negative
reserves).

Accordingly, any “property fair value reserve” previously maintained under the provisions of FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate” shall be reclassified or another effect of the change as of the date of transition (e.g., due to change from cost model to fair value model or vice versa) be applied appropriately as follows:

a.           as a part of retained earnings, insofar as the same relates to the common shareholders;

b.           as a reserve being part of respective class of quasi-equity, insofar as the same relates to them; and

In principle, a change in accounting policy shall only be considered acceptable if it is expected to result in more reliable and relevant information about the effects of transactions, other events or conditions on the institution’s financial position, financial performance or cash flows. Generally, a change from the fair value model to the cost model would not result in a more reliable or relevant presentation. However, in rare circumstances, if an institution justifiably opts to change from fair value model to cost model, such change in accounting policy shall be applied prospectively7. In such case, the fair value of the investment property at the date of change shall be carried as deemed cost. Any “property fair value reserve” for that investment property, shall be transferred to retained earnings / unappropriated profits (or reserves) as attributable respectively to the common shareholders and other participatory stakeholders8.

Presentation and disclosure
An institution shall disclose the following in the financial statements for the period in which FAS 26
“Investment in Real Estate” is withdrawn:

a.           the nature of the change in accounting policy, including a clear statement that it arises from the withdrawal of FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate” along with a brief description of the relevant business model;

b.           the transitional provisions applied, including whether the adjustment has been applied as full restatement or modified restatement approach or prospective approach, along with the justification thereof;

c.           the effect of change in accounting policy, particularly the amount of reclassification from the “property fair value reserve” to retained earnings, in respect of balances attributable to the common shareholders;

d.           the effect of change in accounting policy, particularly the amount and explanation in respect of reclassifications of balances attributable to participatory stakeholders other than common

Effective date

The withdrawal of FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate” shall be effective for the purpose of the financial statements of the institutions for the periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027. It is
permitted to apply such withdrawal of FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate” at an earlier date.

Appendix B: Brief history of the preparation of the statement

H1         The AAOIFI Accounting Board (AAB or “the board”) initiated a comprehensive review of the AAOIFI Financial Accounting Standards (FASs) in 2018, with the objective of aligning all standards with global best practices. The project commenced with the revision of the AAOIFI Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting. Subsequently, the board conducted a systematic review of all AAOIFI FASs to identify areas requiring enhancement or replacement or requiring issuance of new FASs.

H2         AAB held its 25th meeting on 24 Muharram 1443H, corresponding to 2-3 September 2021 and 26th meeting on 28-29 Rabi’ I 1443H, corresponding to 3-4 December 2021, and deliberated on the need to withdraw or revise the existing FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate”. The board advised the working group to evaluate and recommend the way forward.

H3         The working group in its second meeting held on 24 Rabi’ II 1446H, corresponding to 27 October
2024 and 3rd meeting held on 26 Ramadan 1446H corresponding to, 26 March 2025, had considered revising the standard. However, after detailed deliberations the working group recommended to the board the withdrawal of the standard. The working group further recommended the issuance of a short guidance note / statement to clarify specific areas of concern, particularly those relating to transitional matters.

H4         The board in its 42nd meeting held on 24-25 Shawal 1446H, corresponding to 22-23 April 2025, considered the recommendation of the working group. In line with its policy to align with generally accepted accounting principles, unless impeded by Shari'ah considerations or justified by a distinct business model, the board resolved to withdraw FAS 26 “Investment in Real Estate.” The board further decided to issue a formal statement announcing the withdrawal, effective from 1 January
2027, together with transitional guidance to facilitate the withdrawal.

H5         The draft statement was presented for the approval in the AAOIFI Accounting Board’s meeting No.
43rd held on 3-4 Safar 1447H, corresponding to 28-29 July 2025 and was duly approved for obtaining
stakeholders’ input.

H6         A roundtable was held on 4 Rabi’ I 1447H, corresponding to 27 August 2025 to obtain stakeholders’
input.

H7         The board in its 44th meeting held on 30 Rabi II, 1447H, corresponding to 22 October 2025 deliberated on the stakeholders’ comments and approved the statement in principle. However, in respect of a specific matter, i.e, as to whether changing from fair value model to cost model should be allowed, decided to hold a focus group discussion of audit practitioners and experts to have further consultation.

H8         The focus group discussion was held on 13 Jumada II, 1447H, corresponding to 4 December 2025.
After incorporating its suggested conclusion, the board approved the statement by circulation on 9
Rajab, 1447H, corresponding to 29 December 2025.
