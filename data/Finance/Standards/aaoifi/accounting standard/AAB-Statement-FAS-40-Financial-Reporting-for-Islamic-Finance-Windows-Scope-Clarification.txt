AAOIFI Accounting Board (AAB) Statement 1/2026
Financial Accounting Standard (FAS) 40 “Financial Reporting for Islamic
Finance Windows”: Scope Clarification



Objective of the statement

The objective of this statement is to clarify and conditionally exclude certain Islamic finance windows from the scope of AAOIFI Financial Accounting Standard (FAS) 40 “Financial Reporting for Islamic Finance Windows” (the standard).

Statement of scope clarification

AAOIFI Accounting Board (AAB / the board) decided that notwithstanding the generality of the definition of Islamic finance windows, as specified in paragraph 4(b) of the standard, the following may not be3 considered an Islamic finance window in the specific context of the standard:

a.           conventional financial institutions (CFIs) offering limited scope Islamic financial services or executing certain Islamic finance transactions without obtaining an official license for establishing an Islamic finance window, or creating a virtual Islamic finance window (although such practices are not legitimised, encouraged or endorsed by AAOIFI), e.g., in the following scenarios:

i.            whereby a CFI is involved in structuring and advising Shari’ah-compliant transactions including, e.g., investment advisory, Sukuk structuring and syndicated financing (including investing in a Shari’ah-compliant tranche of the same); or

ii.           whereby a CFI is involved in providing limited Shari’ah-compliant transactions to selected existing customer(s) without generally marketing or claiming to offer Shari’ah-compliant products and services; or

b.           CFIs initiating Shari’ah-compliant products and services on a small scale (i.e., not exceeding
5% of the total assets or net profit of the CFI), for a maximum period of five years, either:

i.            in a test-phase; or

ii.           in the normal course of business, provided that there is no public marketing of such products and services (in particular, investment accounts).

[Explanation: It is expected that while exercising such scope exclusions in line with the requirements of this statement, a CFI shall follow the minimum requirements of Shari’ah
principles and rules].



3 Exercise of such option shall be dependent on the assessment of the management and the concurrence of auditors and Shari’ah supervisory board, as well as, the approval of the relevant regulatory and supervisory authority (RSA) where applicable.

Effect of scope clarification on other standards

The requirements of the relevant AAOIFI FASs4 shall be read in conjunction with the scope clarification provided in this statement.

Effective date

This statement shall be effective immediately5.
