Guidance note for Governance Standard (GS) 13 “Waqf Governance” is set out in paragraphs 1–78. This guidance note shall be read in conjunction with AAOIFI’s Shari’ah Standard (SS) 60 on Waqf.

This guidance note shall also be read in conjunction with and in the context of the GS 13. In case of any inconsistencies between this standard and the guidance note, the requirements of the standard shall prevail.

Where the context so requires, the use of masculine gender shall include the feminine gender and the singular shall include the plural and vice versa and the word “person” shall include any institution, corporation, firm, partnership, body corporate or other forms of association. The table of contents and headings do not form part of the text of the standard and are for the purpose of convenience and ease of understanding and may, at times, help in the interpretation of text only.

Introduction

1.           This guidance note includes:

a.           certain explanations on the nature and types of Waqf;
b.           illustrative and suggested terms of references for different committees to be established by those charged with governance / the Nazir; and
c.           suggestions for contents of various significant governance and internal control policies.


2.           This guidance note shall be read in conjunction with and in the context of, the GS 13 “Waqf Governance”. In case of any inconsistencies between the standard and this guidance note, the requirements of the standard shall prevail.

Part A: What is Waqf?

3.           Waqf (for singular and plural) is making a property / source of wealth inalienable and donating its usufruct to beneficiaries.

Charity, private and joint Waqf

4.           Where the benefits of a Waqf are paid to general charitable activities, it is a charitable Waqf.
Where the beneficiaries of the Waqf are private (a close group), it is considered a private Waqf. In cases where the beneficiaries are both public and private, it is considered a joint Waqf.

5.           Where the beneficiaries’ entitlement hierarchy ends, thereafter the Waqf benefits will be directed to general public beneficiaries as determined by the Waqif, or as decided by the Nazir (unless the competent authority decides otherwise).

6.           The Waqf is a family Waqf if the beneficiaries are the members of the immediate or extended family of the Waqif. It is to be noted that in a family Waqf, the Waqif should adhere to the principles of inheritance and shall not specify conditions that are contrary to the Shari’ah principles and rules.

7.           It is recommended that the Waqif should not limit the allocation of the Waqf yield (Ghallah) to his offsprings, rather he should make it a joint Waqf with public entities. It is preferable that the Waqf deed – in a family and joint Waqf – gives the beneficiaries the right to view the annual report prepared by the Nazir, which should include the main elements mentioned in paragraph 79 of GS 13 “Waqf Governance”.

Juristic personality of a Waqf

8.           From a Shari'ah perspective, a Waqf should have an independent juristic personality and a separate financial edict from the Waqif, the beneficiaries and the Nazir. This is to avoid any
constraints as per the respective jurisdiction’s laws and regulations.



4
9.           Upon establishment of a Waqf, the measures and procedures implemented to maintain the Waqf should be in line with the laws of the respective jurisdiction. This may take different legal forms according to the respective jurisdiction’s laws and regulations – such as a Waqf, a trust, an endowment, a foundation, a cooperative society, a company, or a corporation, etc..

Nature of Waqf assets and their selection criteria

10.        Waqf assets should typically be sustainable and long-term in nature so as to achieve greater and continuous benefits.

11.        Waqf assets should be owned by the Waqif (ownership as defined legally and by Shari’ah) at the time of creation of Waqf.

12.        The choice of assets to be endowed for the Waqf is the absolute discretion of the Waqif.

13.        The assets chosen for the purpose of creation of Waqf must be Shari'ah-compliant.

Fulfillment of the conditions of a Waqf

14.        The purpose and conditions set out by the Waqif in the Waqf deed should be the basis for the conduct of the Nazir. The purpose and conditions should not violate the Shari’ah principles and rules and any Waqf-related laws and regulations of the jurisdiction.

15.        Where the conditions of the Waqf deed are explicit, they should be adhered to. In case of ambiguity, it is necessary to identify the intention of the Waqif – it is possible to use customary practices in this regard. Where there are conflicts in the conditions of the Waqf deed but all of them can be achieved, these should be combined. Where this is not possible, it is necessary to implement what is in the best interest of the Waqf.

16.        It is necessary to adhere to the conditions of the Waqf deed, unless, in the best interest of the Waqf and in the achievement of its fundamental objectives, there is an imperative need that requires the Nazir to deviate from such condition(s). Any deviation should not, under any circumstances, contravene the Shari’ah principles and rules, or the Waqf-related laws and regulations of the jurisdiction.

Part B: The board of Nazirs

Mechanism for selection of members

17.        The Waqif shall either appoint the members of the board of Nazirs or determine the eligibility criteria for their selection as well as conditions for dismissal.

18.        In cases where the Waqif does not have the authority to appoint the members, the members will be appointed by a resolution of the board based on a recommendation by the nomination and remuneration committee.

19.        Candidates for membership of the board of Nazirs shall be selected through a standard mechanism on the basis of the Waqf deed, according to their competence, integrity and commitment (of time and attention) to the Waqf and the functions of the board.

5
20.        The Waqif has the authority to reconstitute the board, periodically or when there is a need.
In the absence of the Waqif, the board of Nazirs, must fill in the casual vacancy(ies) created in the board of Nazirs, for any reason.

Suggested criteria for selection of members

21.        Unless the Waqf deed specifies otherwise, the following criteria should be considered in determining the experience and skills of the members of the board whereby they shall:

a.           possess Shari’ah and legal capacity, as well as, appropriate qualifications, diversified knowledge, practical, professional and specialized skills;

b.           be known for piety, justice, honesty and integrity;

c.           be able to dedicate time and effort in supervising the Waqf’s core functions and
activities in order to achieve the objectives of the Waqf;

d.           Collectively be aware of the provisions of the Waqf and the relevant laws and regulations;

e.           possess reasonable management experience;

f.            be fully aware of the Waqf and risk activities that its financial position might be exposed to. Preferably each member should have prior experience in managing a business or a charitable initiative, in one of the areas that the Waqf manages or invests in;

g.           not have filed a bankruptcy application or convicted of default on debt;

h.           ensure integrity, the member has not been previously banned from working by a decision of any of the official authorities in his jurisdiction;

i.            have not been previously banned or suspended from any charity or other establishment;

j.            have not been convicted of any crime or subjected to disciplinary action (including violation of any professional or ethical standards) by a court of law or a professional body for integrity issues; and

k.           have not been convicted of providing incorrect or misleading information to any other party; and

l.            be competent and follow due care in the performance of his functions.

Nomination of chairman and deputy chairman and their functions

22.        Unless the Waqif chooses to nominate a chairman, the members of the board of Nazirs shall choose among themselves a chairman, in line with an election process to be approved by the board of Nazirs, beforehand. Process for election of deputy chairman shall also be specified
therein.

6
23.        The functions and responsibilities of the chairman of the board include, but are not limited to, the following:

a.           agree on meeting dates with the members of the board well in advance e.g at the beginning of each year, as well as the dates of other relevant meetings and ensure that meetings are held on time;

b.           convene meetings of the board and supervise the preparation of the agenda items to be discussed at each meeting. The members of the board have the right to request the inclusion of specific agenda items for a meeting;

c.           chair board meetings including managing the meeting and enabling members to participate effectively;

d.           ensure that the tasks entrusted to one or more of the board members are assigned to the most suitable member(s) able to accomplish the task in the best possible manner;

e.           ensure that the board discusses the main issues affecting the Waqf and makes decisions in a timely manner;

f.            engage in discussions by asking questions, encouraging other board members to participate actively and assisting members to reach sound resolutions;

g.           review the board's performance;

h.           provide guidance for the preparation of training programs to enhance the knowledge and skills of the board members;

i.            ensure that new members are aware of the functions of the board and the areas relevant for the Waqf; and

j.            ensure that members of the board and its various committees are familiar with the rules governing the Waqf.

Functions of the deputy chairman of the board

24.        The deputy chairman shall assist the chairman of the board in his duties, as directed by him.
His duties and responsibilities shall include the following:

a.           carry out the responsibilities of the chairman in his absence or temporary illness, which renders him incapable of assuming his functions or in the event of the termination of the chairman's membership until an alternate is appointed; and

b.           assume any special tasks delegated to him by the chairman of the board.







7
Responsibilities of members of the board

25.        The main duties and responsibilities of the members of the board shall be as follows:

a.           approve the selection of the fund / property manager of the Waqf;

b.           report to the relevant regulator (where relevant), to the Waqif and to the beneficiaries on annual basis the recent developments (within a year), including the financial statements of the Waqf(s);

c.           attend meetings of the board and the relevant committees, participate effectively in discussions and provide independent, unbiased professional views;

d.           attend board meetings readily prepared and updated by reading all the background information and agenda documents provided;

e.           request, as deemed necessary, information related to the Waqf issues and matters to assist the board in making informed decisions;

f.            pay attention to and scrutinize, any indicators of potential problems or risks;

g.           give due consideration to the compliance of the Waqf activities with Shari'ah principles and rules;

h.           not misuse membership in the board for any purposes unrelated to the Waqf service(s);

i.            maintain the confidentiality of all information related to the work of the board;

j.            complete the annual appraisal of the board objectively;

k.           allocate sufficient time to fulfill the board responsibilities;

l.            review the minutes of the meetings and ensure that they include the main discussions that took place in the meeting;

m.         be committed to safeguarding the Waqf’s interests to the exclusion of their personal
and / or professional interests and adhering to the policy of conflict of interest; and n.           inform the board of any relevant issues that may affect the board’s performance.
Termination of membership of the board

26.        The membership of a board member may be terminated in the following cases:

a.           death of the member;

b.           end of the term of appointment of the member;

c.           resignation of the member, provided that the resignation is approved by the Waqif or the board;


8
d.           loss of the member's eligibility as stipulated in the Waqf deed;

e.           dismissal of the member by the Waqif or by a court of law;

f.            a serious breach by the member in discharging the duties and responsibilities assigned to him or misuse of the Waqf funds, provided that the matter is decided by a court of law or the competent authority in the jurisdiction or alternatively a decision of the board which is approved by a two-third majority of the members;
and

g.           dismissal of the member by the board according to the mechanisms and procedures proposed by the nomination and renumeration committee and approve by the board.

27.        The member shall be notified of the termination which shall be effective from the time of the notification.

28.        The board shall take the appropriate measures to stop a dismissed member from continuing to portray himself as a member of the board.

Removal of the Nazir1

29.        The Waqif or the competent authority may suspend and remove the Nazir in the following cases, inter alia, subject to the requirements of Shari’ah principles and rules and that of relevant laws of the jurisdiction:

a.           if the Nazir is found guilty of negligence and misconduct or of violating Shari’ah
principles and rules and / or ethics;

b.           if the Nazir becomes insolvent;

c.           if the Nazir suffers from mental or physical illness that prevents him from carrying out his responsibilities; and

d.           if the Nazir violates the law of the jurisdiction or the Waqf deed or the conditions of the Waqif.

30.        The Waqif or the competent authority, as per law, may also choose to give the Nazir a written / verbal warning. However, a warning may be avoided if the breach is significant and immediate removal is necessary. The Nazir shall be given the opportunity to be heard before suspension or removal.

31.        The Waqif or the competent authority as per law shall carefully examine all aspects and evaluate the situation in order to make an informed decision of removal.





1 The term Nazir implies all the different forms of Nazir, including a board of Nazirs, an individual Nazir, a state
Nazir or a juristic person Nazir.

Replacement and succession policy of members of the board

32.        The board shall adopt a policy of replacement and rotation among its members. The maximum term for a member of the board of Nazirs shall be five years, unless otherwise prescribed by law.

Continuing professional education of the board members

33.        All members of the board shall be encouraged to attend training programs, workshops and conferences relevant to the Waqf and / or its various activities and programs for the continuous development of their knowledge and skills and to be aware of any relevant developments in the Waqf’s functions and operations.

Meetings of the board

34.        The board of Nazirs shall meet on a regular basis in accordance with the plan set in the Waqf’s policies. The chairman shall convene the meeting on a date and place announced in advance and in accordance with the relevant arrangements.

35.        Meetings of the board should be of a reasonable number depending on the specific nature and size of the Waqf. It is recommended that the board of Nazirs of a typical Waqf shall preferably meet at least 3 times a year.

36.        All information on any issue to be submitted to or a decision to be made by, the board shall be provided to the members well in advance, ideally 2-3 weeks before the meeting date.

37.        The meeting shall be attended by more than half of the members of the board either in person or by means of visual communication, where appropriate, provided that the member is able to hear the proceedings of the meeting clearly and without interruption and that the member is present until the end of the meeting. Members attending by means of visual communication shall be entitled to vote on the decisions of the board in the same way as those physically present.

38.        It is preferable not to make decisions by circulation except in cases where the meeting is unable to convene in the usual manner. Decisions by circulation shall be limited to emergencies.

39.        Subject to the laws and regulations of the jurisdiction and the terms and conditions of the Waqf deed, decisions to make any changes to the Waqf assets (other than the Waqf corpus) should be made with the recommendation of the investment committee (if any).

40.        The board may establish any number of committees (composed of members of the board of Nazirs or others), preferably with no less than three and no more than five members, depending on the size and operations of each Waqf and the responsibilities of the
committees.






10
41.        The committees shall be established in accordance with the general procedures established by the board. These shall include defining the functions of the committees, their terms of reference, member selection criteria, authority granted, etc.

42.        The board shall delegate to each committee powers to perform its functions in such a manner as to ensure its effectiveness and to achieve the objectives of the Waqf.

43.        The majority of the committee members shall be independent2 and the chairman of each committee shall also be an independent member.

44.        The committees may seek the help of external consultants to assist them with their functions, as and when required.

Nomination and remuneration committee

45.        The board may, when necessary, establish a nomination and remuneration committee to undertake the following functions:

a.           propose a mechanism for the selection of members of the board of Nazirs and senior management and related procedures;

b.           review the structure, size and composition of the board on a regular basis and prepare recommendations to the board any amendments it deems appropriate;

c.           prepare the eligibility criteria and job descriptions for the vacant positions required to be filled by the Waqf;

d.           ensure adherence to procedures and plans for replacement and succession;

e.           propose the remuneration, if any, to be received by the members of the board and its committees and the remuneration, allowances and benefits of the senior management, within the percentage allocated from the yield (Ghallah) according to the conditions of the Waqf deed or according to customary practice;

f.            conduct periodic evaluation of the performance of the board, its committees, members and senior management and determine the training needs of the members; and

g.           managing / supervising all other tasks as assigned by the board.

Investment and risk management committee
46.        Where necessary, the board of Nazirs may establish an investment and risk management committee and define its structure.



2   An independent member is a member who does not take part in the management function of the Waqf, is not involved in any business relationship with the Waqf and does not have any interest in the benefits of the Waqf.



11
47.        The investment and risk management committee shall have the following functions:

a.           prepare and update periodically an investment strategy and policy to be adopted by the board of Nazirs in a manner that does not contradict with the conditions of the Waqf deed. The investment objectives, the criteria and financial management shall be aligned with the objectives / purpose for which the Waqf was established;

b.           manage the assets / investments of the Waqf with the objective of capital preservation, while emphasizing yield (Ghallah) generation in order to support spending requirements in line with the Waqf deed;

c.           analyze investment opportunities and recommend them to the board (after verifying their alignment with the Waqf strategy, investment policy and compliance to
Shari’ah principles and rules);

d.           advise the board in all matters related to investments;

e.           assess the investment risk policy in coordination with the relevant committee and make appropriate recommendations and mitigation plans to the board of Nazirs; and

f.            approve the selection and dismissal criteria for external investment (and investment property) managers and undertake their performance appraisal.

Executive committee

48.        Where necessary and for a period not exceeding the duration of its term, the board of Nazirs may establish an executive committee, consisting of at least three members, where at least one third of them shall be members of the board and two thirds members of the senior management. The board may appoint a chairperson or delegate to the members of the committee to choose a chairperson among them.

49.        The executive committee shall have the following functions:

a.           develop the Waqf’s organizational structure and submit it to the board for approval;

b.           develop the authority matrix and submit it to the board for approval;

c.           supervise and monitor the operational and financial performance of the Waqf;

d.           exercise the financial and administrative powers entrusted to the committee by the board;

e.           liaise with the investment committee on new investment projects;

f.            all other tasks assigned to it by the board.

50.        The executive committee shall meet on a regular basis and the number of its meetings shall depend on the size and nature of the Waqf and the responsibilities of the committee.


12
Governance committee

51.        The board may establish a governance committee, consisting of three or more members, including at least one independent member, who shall be the chairman of the committee.

52.        The governance committee shall have the following functions:

a.           prepare and update the Waqf governance guidelines, in line with leading practices and monitor their execution – working / collaborating with the nomination and remuneration committee, the Shari'ah supervisory board (SSB) and the senior management in this regard;

b.           develop and / or review the code of professional conduct, which represents the values of the Waqf and other internal policies and procedures to meet the needs of the Waqf deed and conform to best practices;

c.           provide the board of Nazirs with reports and recommendations, including assessment of compliance with the Waqf governance guidelines;

d.           brief the board of Nazirs on the latest governance-related developments, decisions and laws issued by the various regulatory bodies and encouraged by volunteer groups; and

e.           managing / supervising all related tasks assigned to it by the board of Nazirs.

Periodic evaluation of the performance of the board, its committees and members

53.        The board of Nazirs is responsible for the periodic evaluation of the performance of the boards’ committees and its members. This is in order to improve the board’s performance and determine the training needs of its members. The board may use specialized bodies to conduct such an assessment.

54.        The nomination and remuneration committee is responsible to prepare a clear and transparent mechanism to evaluate the performance of the committees and the board as a whole, the performance of each of the members and that of the senior managers. This evaluation shall be carried out according to quantitative and qualitative key performance indicators (KPIs) prepared by the nomination and remuneration committee and approved by the board of Nazirs.

Part C: Pertinent policies and procedures for governance and internal control

Policy for stakeholders’ relationship

55.        The Waqf shall develop and follow a uniform communication policy, in line with market best practices, to govern the relationship with stakeholders including the public at large.

56.        The policy shall aim to uphold the principles of governance as defined in Waqf governance standard.



13
57.        Those charged with governance shall review the communication policy periodically and update it as necessary.

Conflict of interest policy

58.        The board of Nazirs shall approve a policy to address conflict of interest, if any, which shall cover the following aspects:

a.           situations involving conflict of interest;

b.           procedures to be followed in the event of conflict of interest;

c.           requirements related to the disclosure of conflict of interest (potential or actual);
and

d.           necessary course of action in conflict of interest cases.

59.        The policy is intended to ensure that all parties affiliated with the Waqf perform their duties and responsibilities in a sincere and transparent manner towards achieving the interests of the Waqf in all circumstances, to the exclusion of their personal or corporate interests.

Whistle-blowing policy

60.        The whistle-blowing policy shall require all employees and affiliates to internally report and / or escalate any suspected violations against the regulations, policies or rules related to the Waqf, in a prescribed manner so that the interests of the Waqf are protected from any adverse consequences. The policy shall include:

a.           procedures by which a party may report a flagrant breach (or a high probability of such breach) of the regulations, policies and rules governing the Waqf;

b.           assignment of responsibility to the department receiving such report, information and complaint; and

c.           procedures for the confidentiality of the identity of the whistle-blower / complainant and protection against any harassment or other consequences that may be suffered as a result of the report / complaint.

Document archiving policy

61.        Documents have legal importance to protect the Waqf and its rights. It is necessary to preserve these documents and records in both paper and electronic formats, while ensuring their confidentiality. The document archiving policy shall cover the following:

a.           types of documents to be archived;

b.           mechanism for the classification of documents;

c.            duration of archiving;

d.           access control protocol;

14
e.           storage and retrieval of documents; and f.            safe disposal of documents.
Complaints policy

62.        The board of Nazirs shall adopt a complaints policy to raise the quality of the work of the
Waqf and comply with the ISO standard No. 10002: 2014 (or any other equivalent
standards). In this regard, it is recommended to appoint a compliance officer, even if it is not required by the relevant regulator, who shall be responsible for assuring compliance and maintaining the stakeholders’ confidence in the Waqf.

External communication policy

63.        Given the importance of communication, especially in the age of social media, the board of
Nazirs shall adopt a policy covering the following:

a.           the objectives and the strategy of the Waqf for media communication and external parties;

b.           criteria for selecting suitable means of communication with the beneficiaries and the broader community of the Waqf and appropriate procedures for such communication;

c.           responsibilities of the concerned department for media communication including the protocol of media contact and appointment of the spokesperson; and

d.           training of relevant employees in media relations.

Information security policy

64.        The Nazir shall adopt an information security policy and maintain information confidentiality.

65.         The requirements of ISO standard No. 27001: 2013 (or any other equivalent standards)
should be taken into consideration in the preparation of this policy.

Human resource policy

66.        The board of Nazirs shall adopt a human resource policy that, taking into account the market conditions, will enable the Waqf to attract the best talent and expertise to achieve its objectives.

67.        This policy shall take into consideration the following:

a.           attracting, training, developing and retaining staff;

b.           maintaining a healthy work environment – governed by clarity, meritocracy, equality, professionalism and unlawful discriminatory practices, including harassment;


15
c.           establishing a fair and robust system of incentives and disincentives based on performance;

d.           establishing an effective and fair complaint redressal system; and e.           establishing arrangements for succession planning.
Code of professional conduct

68.        A code of professional conduct shall be adopted by all members of the board of Nazirs and the management and shall be principally in line with the ‘AAOIFI’s Code of Ethics for Islamic Finance Professionals’.

69.        This code shall contain the following:

a.           commitment to achieving the Waqf vision, mission, goals and values;

b.           emphasis on working honestly, faithfully and professionally;

c.           ensuring confidentiality of information related to the Waqf;

d.           commitment to work transparently and avoid conflict of interest;

e.           compliance with laws, regulations, internal policies and good governance practices;
and

f.            commitment to support the beneficiaries of the Waqf.

Part D: Specific provisions for different types of Nazirs

The board of Nazirs

70.        The establishment of a board of Nazirs shall be the preferred option for most Waqf, after due consideration to the nature and respective size of resources and operations.

71.        The Waqif shall establish a board of Nazirs and determine its principal rights, responsibilities and terms of reference in the Waqf deed.

72.        Typically, a board of Nazirs shall consist of not less than three members and not more than nine members. It should be ensured that there are a reasonable number of members who are independent of the Waqf, the Waqif and other Nazirs. Representation may also be provided to the beneficiaries through a fair and transparent mechanism.

Individual Nazir

73.        A Waqif may appoint an individual as the Nazir of the Waqf. In this case, the Waqif shall determine the selection criteria for such individual including the qualifications, experience and skill-set requirements.

74.        The Waqif shall also specify the successor or the succession mechanism for the Nazir.

Juristic person Nazir

75.        A Waqif may appoint a juristic person (institution) as the Nazir of the Waqf. In this case,
those charged with governance of such institution shall take the responsibility of the Nazir of the Waqf.

76.        Such institution may assign the governance and management responsibilities of the Waqf to their own management or to selected personnel amongst their management or alternatively, may appoint a dedicated (preferably independent) management for the Waqf at their discretion.

State Nazir

77.        A Waqif may appoint the state as the Nazir of the Waqf or the state may acquire the Nazir rights and responsibilities by virtue of the stipulations of the Waqf deed or by legal action, etc..

78.        In situations where the Waqf is in governance of the state Nazir, it is preferable that instead of the direct supervision model of the Waqf, the state shall form a supplementary board of Nazirs and supervise its functioning. In such a situation, the supplementary board of Nazirs (by whatever name it is called) shall have the same rights and responsibilities as a board of Nazirs under this standard, subject to any conditions and limitations as may be specified by the state for this purpose. A single supplementary board of Nazirs may be appointed by the state, along with support staff, to manage the affairs of multiple Waqf under the governance
of state Nazir.
